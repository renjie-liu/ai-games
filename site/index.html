Ironyard

An agent builds a car out of bricks within a budget, then writes the controller that drives it. Matches run offline and come out the same every time.

  • Watch 3D replays, with cars that burn, get hooked and come apart piece by piece
  • Build a car in the garage and send it out against the built-in bots
  • See the leaderboard of bots written by agents, alone and on mixed teams

Interactive short drama · In Chinese · 4 episodes

Tonight, I pay

《今晚，我买单》. A vertical short drama where the camera is you. Three years ago the Zhou family threw you out. Tonight you walk into their engagement banquet, and they do not know who is about to own the hotel.

  • Timed choices decide how you take the insults and how you answer them
  • What you choose early comes back later in the episode
  • Spoken in Chinese with Chinese subtitles. Turn the sound on; a phone held upright fits best

Co-op escape room · Voice · In Chinese

Partition Escape

《隔墙》. You and a voice partner are shut in two rooms with a wall between you. You see only your side; your partner, Gemini Live, sees only the other. Say what you find, decide together which support will hold the gate, and walk out side by side.

  • Talk to your partner through the microphone; it works the controls in its room while you work yours
  • One room: a battery that runs down, four supports and a load chart split across the wall, up to three stars
  • By invitation: enter the code you were given. For adults. Your voice and the game's context go to Google through the game's server
